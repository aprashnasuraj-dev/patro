Hourly to Salary Converter with Unpaid Time Off

Convert an hourly wage into annual, monthly and pay-period gross pay while explicitly subtracting unpaid time off and optionally including overtime. Normal use stays in this browser and sends no tool input to a server.

Work pattern

What this tool does

Convert an hourly wage into annual, monthly and pay-period gross pay while explicitly subtracting unpaid time off and optionally including overtime.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

At $25/hour, 40 hours/week, 10 unpaid 8-hour days and 2 overtime hours/week, the tool shows gross annual pay and the cost of unpaid time.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Gross wage conversion only; unpaid time reduces regular hours; overtime is paid at the entered multiplier.

Limitations

Benefits, taxes, bonuses, shift premiums and employer-specific payroll calendars are excluded unless represented by inputs.

FAQ

Does this include benefits or taxes?

No. It converts gross wage cash flow only.
