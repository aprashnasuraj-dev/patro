Loan cash flows

What this tool does

Estimate a closed-end loan's cash-flow APR after upfront and financed fees using equal monthly payments and an implied monthly rate.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

A $10,000 loan with $250 deducted upfront and $100 financed produces different net proceeds and payment cash flows, from which an implied monthly rate is solved.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Single advance, equal monthly periods, equal payments, fees exactly as entered; no legal fee classification.

Limitations

Not a legal APR disclosure engine; irregular dates, multiple advances and regulatory fee classification are unsupported.

FAQ

Is this a Regulation Z compliance APR?

No. It is a transparent equal-period cash-flow estimate and flags cases outside that scope.
