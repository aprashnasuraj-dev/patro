Savings Goal Calculator (Date or Contribution)

Solve either the monthly contribution needed to hit a savings goal by a target horizon or the time needed for a fixed contribution to reach the goal. Normal use stays in this browser and sends no tool input to a server.

Savings target

What this tool does

Solve either the monthly contribution needed to hit a savings goal by a target horizon or the time needed for a fixed contribution to reach the goal.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The sample can solve the monthly contribution needed to move from $5,000 to $30,000 in 36 months at the entered return.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Monthly compounding; contribution occurs at month end; rate stays constant.

Limitations

No taxes, fees, contribution timing variants or stochastic returns.

FAQ

Does the return happen every month?

The projection converts the annual input to a simple monthly rate and compounds monthly.
