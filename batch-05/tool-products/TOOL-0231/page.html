Marginal Tax Bracket Calculator with Editable Brackets

Apply a user-editable progressive bracket table to taxable income and show total tax, marginal rate and effective rate without assuming a jurisdiction. Normal use stays in this browser and sends no tool input to a server.

Income and editable brackets

Hypothetical sample only. One line per bracket: upper limit, rate %. Use “max” for the last bracket.

What this tool does

Apply a user-editable progressive bracket table to taxable income and show total tax, marginal rate and effective rate without assuming a jurisdiction.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The hypothetical sample taxes slices of taxable income at 10%, 20% and 30%; it is deliberately not a real jurisdiction table.

Supported formats / units

Bracket lines use upper-limit,rate-percent; use 'max' for the final open-ended bracket.

Assumptions

Progressive brackets are applied in ascending order to income after entered deductions.

Limitations

No jurisdiction, filing status, credits, phase-outs or special taxes are built in.

FAQ

Are these official tax brackets?

No. The sample is hypothetical; you must enter the brackets that apply to your use case.
