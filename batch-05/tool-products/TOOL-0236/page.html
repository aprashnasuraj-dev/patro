DRIP scenario

What this tool does

Model a dividend-reinvestment scenario with user-set dividend yield/growth, share-price growth and payment frequency, showing shares accumulated and ending value.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The sample reinvests a 3% starting dividend yield quarterly while dividend/share and share price grow at separate entered rates.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Dividend yield is converted to annual dividend per share at the starting price, then dividend/share and price grow independently.

Limitations

Dividends, dividend growth and share prices can fall; taxes and brokerage constraints are excluded.

FAQ

Does DRIP guarantee higher returns?

No. The tool only compounds the dividend and price-growth assumptions you enter.
