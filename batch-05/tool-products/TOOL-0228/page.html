Card payoff

What this tool does

Estimate how long a credit-card balance takes to repay under a fixed monthly payment and APR, with explicit detection of negative amortization.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The sample tests a $7,500 balance at 22.9% APR with a fixed $350 monthly payment and no new charges.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Monthly interest and fixed payment; optional new charges are added monthly; no late fees or variable APR.

Limitations

Issuer minimum-payment formulas, fees, compounding conventions and rate changes can differ.

FAQ

Why can payoff be impossible?

If payment does not cover interest plus new charges, the balance does not decline.
