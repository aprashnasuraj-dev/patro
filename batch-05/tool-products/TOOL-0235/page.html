Cost Basis Calculator (FIFO and Average) from Trade CSV

Parse a bounded trade CSV locally and compute mechanical FIFO and moving-average cost-basis results, with a warning that tax eligibility varies by asset and jurisdiction. Normal use stays in this browser and sends no tool input to a server.

Trade CSV

Required columns: date,symbol,action,quantity,price. Optional: fees. Actions: BUY, SELL, DRIP. Maximum 10,000 rows / 2 MB.

What this tool does

Parse a bounded trade CSV locally and compute mechanical FIFO and moving-average cost-basis results, with a warning that tax eligibility varies by asset and jurisdiction.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The sample buys 100 ABC shares at $10, then 50 at $12, then sells 80 at $15 to show the difference between FIFO and pooled average basis.

Supported formats / units

UTF-8 CSV, comma-delimited, required header fields date,symbol,action,quantity,price; optional fees; ISO date YYYY-MM-DD.

Assumptions

Trades are processed by date then file order; fees are added to buys/DRIP basis and subtracted from sell proceeds.

Limitations

Not tax filing software; corporate actions, wash sales, specific identification and jurisdiction-specific elections are unsupported.

FAQ

Can I use average basis for any stock?

No. Eligibility depends on asset and jurisdiction; the tool labels average basis as a mechanical comparison.
