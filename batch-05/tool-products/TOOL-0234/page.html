What this tool does

Compare renting and buying over a chosen horizon using transparent home, mortgage, recurring-cost, appreciation, rent-growth and investment-return assumptions.

How to use it

Review the prefilled example, replace it with your values, run the calculation, then read the assumptions and limitations before copying or exporting any result.

Worked example

The sample compares a $400,000 home with 20% down against $2,200 monthly rent over seven years using editable ownership and growth assumptions.

Supported formats / units

Currency values are unit-agnostic unless a specific jurisdiction is stated; percentages are entered as percent values (for example 7.5 for 7.5%).

Assumptions

Same starting cash budget for renter and buyer; monthly cash-flow differences are invested at the entered return.

Limitations

A scenario model, not a forecast; taxes, financing changes, renovations, deposits and many transaction-specific costs are omitted.

FAQ

Does this include tax deductions?

No. Tax effects are intentionally excluded unless you model them separately.
