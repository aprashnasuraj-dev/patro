What this tool does

Compare playback support reported by the live browser for video and audio MIME strings.

How to use it

Add up to 32 codec MIME entries, one per line, and inspect canPlayType and MediaSource signals.

Worked example

video/mp4; codecs="avc1.42E01E" may report probably, maybe or empty.

Supported inputs & assumptions

Browser HTMLMediaElement.canPlayType and MediaSource.isTypeSupported, when available.

Known limitations

Support claims do not guarantee actual successful decoding, hardware acceleration or DRM.
