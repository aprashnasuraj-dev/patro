What this tool does

Inspect Received hops, Authentication-Results claims, and suspicious header anomalies locally.

How to use it

Paste headers through the first blank line, then review the parsed route and warnings.

Worked example

Authentication-Results: mx.example; spf=pass is a reported claim, not independent proof.

Supported inputs & assumptions

RFC 5322 header fields with folding, and reported SPF/DKIM/DMARC status.

Known limitations

Cannot establish origin authenticity, validate cryptography or prove sender identity.
