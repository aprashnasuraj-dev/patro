VAT/GST Registration Threshold Tracker

Compare turnover with a dated reference set of VAT/GST registration thresholds and expose each jurisdiction's measurement window.

How this calculator works

Compare turnover with a dated reference set of VAT/GST registration thresholds and expose each jurisdiction's measurement window. Inputs are processed in your browser. Normal use does not upload the values you enter to a server or third party.

This is an educational planning tool, not tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice. Confirm current rules and your facts before filing or registering.

Assumptions and limits

The calculator exposes important assumptions as editable inputs where practical. Standards, tax thresholds, material tables and jurisdiction rules can change, so source dates and scope limits are retained with the tool package.

Privacy and safety

Do not enter secrets, account credentials, taxpayer identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Results are estimates until independently verified.
