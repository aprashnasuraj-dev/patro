G-code Print Time and Filament Estimator

Estimate G-code motion time, extrusion length, material mass, and cost entirely in the browser.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Parses common G0/G1, feedrate, positioning, extrusion modes and G4 dwell.

How to use it

Paste bounded G-code and material properties.

Worked example

Extruding 9.6 mm of 1.75 mm filament yields volume from filament cross-section × length.

Assumptions

F is interpreted as units/minute; E is treated as filament length unless volumetric extrusion is used.

Limitations

Acceleration/jerk, heater waits, firmware planner effects, arcs/macros and volumetric extrusion are not fully simulated.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

Marlin G0/G1 semantics.
