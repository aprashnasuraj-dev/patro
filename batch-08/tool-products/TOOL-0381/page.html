Resistor Color Code Calculator (4, 5, 6 Band, Both Directions)

Decode and encode common 4-, 5-, and 6-band resistor markings, including tolerance and optional temperature coefficient.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Translates resistor color bands to resistance and encodes numeric resistance into the nearest representable band pattern.

How to use it

Enter comma-separated colors to decode, or enter resistance/tolerance and choose 4 or 5 bands to encode.

Worked example

brown, black, black, red, brown → 10 kΩ ±1%.

Assumptions

Standard digit, multiplier and common tolerance mappings are used. Six-band decode treats band 6 as temperature coefficient.

Limitations

Military/legacy special 5-band schemes and manufacturer-specific markings are outside scope.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.
