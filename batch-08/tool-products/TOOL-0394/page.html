Recipe Scaler with Friendly Fractions

Scale recipe quantities and convert decimal results into friendly fractions to sixteenths.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Parses simple quantity-first ingredient lines and applies a multiplier.

How to use it

Use integers, decimals, simple fractions or mixed fractions.

Worked example

1/2 cup × 1.5 → 3/4 cup.

Assumptions

Friendly fractions round to the nearest 1/16.

Limitations

Units are not converted or dimensionally checked; ambiguous free-form ingredient text remains text.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

Arithmetic scaling only.
