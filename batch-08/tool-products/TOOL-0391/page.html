Hyperfocal Distance and Depth of Field Calculator

Calculate hyperfocal distance and near/far depth-of-field limits using an editable circle of confusion.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Uses the thin-lens hyperfocal/DOF equations.

How to use it

Enter focal length, f-number, circle of confusion and focus distance.

Worked example

35 mm at f/8 with 0.03 mm CoC gives a hyperfocal distance of roughly 5.1 m.

Assumptions

Circle of confusion is a chosen acceptability criterion, not a sensor law.

Limitations

Lens pupil magnification, diffraction, focus breathing and output viewing conditions are simplified.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

Standard geometrical-optics depth-of-field equations.
