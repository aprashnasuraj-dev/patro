What this tool does

Estimate workspace deductions under an explicitly selected US, UK, Canadian or Australian scenario; distinguish a simplified rate from an allocated actual-cost estimate.

How to use

  1. Enter the scenario values shown in the form.
  2. Run the calculation and review the assumptions and warnings.
  3. Copy or export the self-contained estimate for your records.

Worked example

Start with this editable scenario: Tax location: US, Workspace area: 120, Total dwelling area: 1000. Select Calculate to review an explicit breakdown and warnings—not an official legal or tax determination.

Supported input formats and units

numbers / choices, ft², ft², numbers / choices, %. Dates use the Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD civil-date format. Only listed input ranges are supported.

Assumptions and limitations

Employment/self-employment status, allowable expense categories, records and year-specific AU rate are not checked. US part-year square-foot averaging is simplified.

Frequently asked question

Does this result replace an official filing, legal determination or professional safety inspection?

No. The model is a transparent scenario estimate. Confirm your date, local rule, rates and applicable eligibility with a primary authority.
