Bicycle Gear Inches, Gain Ratio and Speed at Cadence Calculator

Calculate bicycle drive ratio, gear inches, gain ratio, and theoretical speed at cadence.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Combines chainring/sprocket ratio with wheel diameter and crank length.

How to use it

Enter tooth counts, effective wheel diameter, crank length and cadence.

Worked example

50×17 on a 26.3-inch wheel at 90 rpm gives road-speed and gearing metrics.

Assumptions

Wheel diameter should be effective rolling diameter; no tire slip is modeled.

Limitations

Real speed changes with tire growth, drivetrain losses, cadence variation and measured rollout.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

Gear inches and gain ratio are mechanical geometry conventions.
