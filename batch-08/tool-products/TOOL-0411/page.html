How this calculator works

Convert city-specific design rainfall and roof area into drainage flow demand, then compare it with a verified downspout capacity. Inputs are processed in your browser. Normal use does not upload the values you enter to a server or third party.

This is a planning calculator. Verify code, product data, legal/tax treatment, and local requirements before acting.

Assumptions and limits

The calculator exposes important assumptions as editable inputs where practical. Standards, tax thresholds, material tables and jurisdiction rules can change, so source dates and scope limits are retained with the tool package.

Privacy and safety

Do not enter secrets, account credentials, taxpayer identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Results are estimates until independently verified.
