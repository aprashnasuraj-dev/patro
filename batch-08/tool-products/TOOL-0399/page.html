Knitting Gauge and Cast-On Calculator

Convert a measured knitting swatch into cast-on stitches while respecting edge stitches and pattern repeats.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Derives stitches per unit from your swatch, scales to target width, then rounds to a repeat multiple plus add-on.

How to use it

Enter measured swatch stitches/width and your target finished width.

Worked example

20 stitches over 10 cm means 2 stitches/cm; 50 cm needs about 100 stitches before repeat adjustments.

Assumptions

Your measured swatch is authoritative for your project.

Limitations

Blocking, stitch pattern, fiber, needle changes, ease and shaping affect finished dimensions.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

Craft Yarn Council defines gauge as stitches/rows per unit and advises matching the pattern gauge via swatching.
