Crop Factor and Equivalent Focal Length Calculator

Calculate crop factor, full-frame-equivalent focal length, depth-of-field-equivalent f-number, and sensor field of view.

Equivalent f-number here refers to depth-of-field framing equivalence; it does not change exposure at the sensor.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Derives crop factor from sensor diagonals and angle of view from sensor size/focal length.

How to use it

Enter sensor dimensions, lens focal length and f-number.

Worked example

APS-C around 23.5×15.6 mm gives roughly 1.5× crop.

Assumptions

36×24 mm is the full-frame reference.

Limitations

DOF-equivalent aperture does not mean equal exposure; entrance pupil and framing assumptions matter.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.
