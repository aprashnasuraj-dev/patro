Homebrew ABV Calculator from OG and FG

Estimate beer alcohol by volume from original and final specific gravity using two common equations.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Calculates the simple 131.25× gravity-drop estimate plus a higher-order alternative.

How to use it

Enter hydrometer-corrected OG and FG.

Worked example

1.055 to 1.012 gives about 5.64% by the simple estimate.

Assumptions

Gravity readings are assumed corrected and fermentation is assumed to reduce gravity.

Limitations

High-gravity beer, refractometer readings, dissolved solids and alcohol/water contraction can require better methods.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

American Homebrewers Association gravity terminology; equations are calculator approximations.
