Coffee Brew Ratio Calculator with Brew Timer

Calculate coffee-to-water ratio and run a local brew timer with equal phase markers.

Result

Enter values and run the tool.

What this tool does

Solves coffee/water ratio and shows grams per liter alongside a simple local timer.

How to use it

Enter coffee and water weights; use 0 for one side to solve at the reference 55 g/L ratio.

Worked example

22 g coffee with 400 g water is about 1:18.18.

Assumptions

Water grams are treated approximately as milliliters for ratio display.

Limitations

Taste, extraction, grinder, roast and brewer geometry are not inferred.

Privacy and security

Normal operation is browser-local. Values and uploaded files are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party.

Authoritative references

SCA Golden Cup guidance cites roughly 55 g/L ±10% as a reference brewing ratio.
