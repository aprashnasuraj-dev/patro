PNG/JPG to AVIF and WebP with Quality Compare

Encode one PNG or JPEG to AVIF and WebP at comparable quality settings and show file-size savings side by side.

Work area

What this tool does

Encode one PNG or JPEG to AVIF and WebP at comparable quality settings and show file-size savings side by side.

How to use it

  1. Choose local input files and review the task-specific controls.
  2. Run the tool. Processing stays in this browser session.
  3. Review warnings and previews before copying or downloading output.

Worked example

Open a 2.4 MB PNG, compare WebP quality 82 and AVIF quality 55, then download the smaller acceptable result.

Supported formats and units

PNG/JPEG input; WebP and AVIF output when the browser or local codec supports them.

Assumptions

Native Canvas encoders are preferred; a bundled AVIF codec can be lazy-loaded if native AVIF encoding is unavailable.

Limitations

Quality numbers are codec-specific and not visually equivalent; users must judge the preview.
