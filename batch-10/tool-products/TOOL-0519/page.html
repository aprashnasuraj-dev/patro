    What this tool does

    Screen six months of household gross income against a user-verified US Trustee median table for the relevant filing date.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    Six $5,000 monthly receipts annualize to $60,000 for initial median screening.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Current monthly income is average of six complete calendar months before filing, ×12; compares against externally verified DOJ state table.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Not a case-filing or legal eligibility decision; statutory exemptions may alter calculation. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.
