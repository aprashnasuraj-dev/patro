Event Space Capacity Calculator

Compare usable event-space area, arrangement load factors and an independently verified occupancy limit without implying fire-code approval.

What it does

Compare usable event-space area, arrangement load factors and an independently verified occupancy limit without implying fire-code approval.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Room length, Room width, Stage, service area and obstacles.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

60×40 ft minus 300 ft² = 2,100 ft²; with table/chair factor 15 ft²/person = 140 factor-based occupants before other restrictions.

Supported inputs and measurement

numeric quantities · option selectors. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

Only net usable area is used; 5/7/15 sq ft net factors are illustrative under ICC reference edition and may not apply locally. Entrances/exits, fixture counts, accessibility, permits, approved maximum and egress reduce capacity.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
