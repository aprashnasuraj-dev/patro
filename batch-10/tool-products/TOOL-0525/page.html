    What this tool does

    Compare delivered-space-heat cost per year for a heat pump versus a gas boiler and show incremental investment payback.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    16,000 kWh heat/3 COP=5,333 kWh electricity; compare 16,000/0.88 kWh gas energy.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Cost_HP = useful heat / seasonal COP × power price; cost_gas = useful heat / boiler efficiency × gas energy price.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Apples-to-apples demand and accurate seasonal metrics required. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.
