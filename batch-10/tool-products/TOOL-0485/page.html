What it does

Reverse-plan BBQ prep, cook, rest and contingency milestones with conservative food-safety thresholds and explicit uncertainty about cooking duration.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Meat/cut, Serve at local date/time, Estimated active cook duration.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

18:00 serving, 8h cook, 60min rest, 90min contingency and 45min prep: preparation starts 06:45 local time.

Supported inputs and measurement

numeric quantities · option selectors · local date and time. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

Cooking duration is user-estimated, not a physical model; actual doneness requires calibrated probe temperature, resting and safe holding. Food should not be kept unsafely in temperature danger zones.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
