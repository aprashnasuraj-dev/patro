    What this tool does

    Check whole-day UK absences against a rolling 12-month ILR screen or nationality total/last-year limits.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    For standard five-year nationality route, normally <=450 total days and <=90 days in final 12 months.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Whole days outside UK counted between departure/return. ILR uses rolling 12 calendar months; citizenship uses 3/5-year total and final 12 months.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Applications with exempt absences, special routes or overlapping trips require review. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.

    References

    UK Continuous Residence Rules UK nationality Form AN guidance

    Is this result a final legal, clinical, tax or engineering determination? No. This tool exposes stated assumptions and its domain-specific exclusions. Confirm the applicable real-world data and requirements before relying on an estimate.
