Limitations and checks

    What this tool does

    Estimate Canadian OAS recovery tax and the incremental effect of an RRIF withdrawal, keeping the income year and payment period explicit.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    2025 net income C$100,000 against C$93,454 threshold implies C$981.90 recovery before benefit cap.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Recovery tax = min(OAS received,15% of annual net world income above threshold); not the total marginal tax.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Non-resident and income-year exceptions, RRIF minimum percentages and pension splitting need external review. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.
