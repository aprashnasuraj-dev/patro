What it does

Calculate cord fractions from a measured, well-stowed stack and cost per million useful BTU using explicitly provided heat content and stove efficiency assumptions.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Stacked wood length, Stacked wood height, Stacked wood depth.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

A measured 8×4×4 ft well-stowed stack is exactly one cord; at $350, 20 million BTU/cord and 70% efficiency, cost is $25 per useful million BTU.

Supported inputs and measurement

numeric quantities. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

128 ft³ cord measures well-stowed stacked wood including air spaces, not solid wood. Species, seasoning/moisture and appliance performance drive BTU figures; values are user-supplied, not authoritative fuel certification.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
