What it does

Use measured catch-weight and calibrated width/distance to estimate spreader delivery versus the bag label rate; never invent a proprietary dial setting.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Spread material, Target product application mass, Full treatment area.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

1.4 kg over 3 m × 20 m gives 2.33 kg per 100 m². Against a 3 kg target, the measured flow is about 22.2% below target.

Supported inputs and measurement

numeric quantities · option selectors. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

Actual width needs overlap correction; walk/vehicle speed and gate characteristics are equipment-specific. Rate ratios are directional only; no universal dial number or seed viability inference.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
