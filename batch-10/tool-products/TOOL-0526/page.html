    What this tool does

    Estimate sustained generator load plus one or more motor starting surges from an appliance roster with explicit simultaneous-start groups.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    200W refrigerator with 1200W start plus 900W pump with 2200W start: staggered peak is lower than simultaneous peak.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Peak = total steady running watts plus the largest simultaneous starting extra watts; applies user-entered reserve.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Does not size fuel tank, neutral bonding, grounding, power factor or transfer switch. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.

    References

    US DOE Energy Saver generators

    Frequently asked question

    Is this result a final legal, clinical, tax or engineering determination? No. This tool exposes stated assumptions and its domain-specific exclusions. Confirm the applicable real-world data and requirements before relying on an estimate.
