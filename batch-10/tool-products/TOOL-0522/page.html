    What this tool does

    Screen eligible flight-arrival delay compensation using distinct EU, UK and Canadian regimes and carrier-control assumptions.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    Canada large airline, controllable 6-hour final arrival delay: CAD 700 compensation band.

    Inputs and assumptions

    EU/UK compensation uses distance bands with long-flight sub-4-hour reduction; Canadian uses arrival delay and carrier size.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Connections, rerouting, claims windows and the airline fault category may materially change compensation. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.

    References

    EU air passenger rights UK CAA delayed flights Canadian Transportation Agency APPR

    Frequently asked question

    Is this result a final legal, clinical, tax or engineering determination? No. This tool exposes stated assumptions and its domain-specific exclusions. Confirm the applicable real-world data and requirements before relying on an estimate.
