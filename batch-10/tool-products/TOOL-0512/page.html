    What this tool does

    Compare first-home savings account remaining room and RRSP Home Buyers’ Plan withdrawal capacity with a repayment preview.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    A new FHSA starts with C$8,000 participation room; HBP withdrawal ceiling is C$60,000 subject to eligibility.

    Inputs and assumptions

    FHSA annual room $8,000 plus up to $8,000 carryforward; $40,000 lifetime combined contributions/transfers. HBP cap $60,000.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Complex excess FHSA repairs, withdrawal year exemptions and RRSP qualification excluded. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.
