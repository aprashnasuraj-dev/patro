    What this tool does

    Calculate an auditable long-service-leave accrual scenario only after entering the relevant state, award or contract rate.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    At 0.8667 weeks per service year, eight years accrue roughly 6.93 weeks before leave already taken.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Accrued weeks = eligible service years × legally verified accrual rate; pay at entered ordinary weekly rate.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    Entitlement vesting and pro-rata payout cannot be inferred solely from service years. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.
