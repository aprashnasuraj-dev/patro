Limitations and checks

    What this tool does

    Compare actual hand-luggage dimensions against explicitly entered airline allowance limits, with configurable dimension-rotation.

    How to use it

    Use the prefilled worked example to understand the inputs. Replace each sample value with values from your own records and authoritative sources. Run the calculation and read all warnings before making a decision.

    Worked example

    A 55×35×23 cm bag fits a 56×36×23 cm box and weighs 8 kg vs 10 kg allowance.

    Inputs and assumptions

    Measured packed bag is compared in cm/kg; optional dimension rotation handles orientation changes.

    Supported cases and exclusions

    No airline-specific policy database or guarantee of boarding acceptance. All values are bounded and malformed inputs are rejected rather than silently converted.

    References

    IATA passenger baggage guidance

    Frequently asked question

    Is this result a final legal, clinical, tax or engineering determination? No. This tool exposes stated assumptions and its domain-specific exclusions. Confirm the applicable real-world data and requirements before relying on an estimate.
