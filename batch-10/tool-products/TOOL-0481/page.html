What it does

Identify plausible shipping carriers from length and prefix without uploading a tracking number. Returns ambiguity and a neutral official tracking-site pointer; no tracking-status API call.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Tracking reference.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

A sample beginning 1Z followed by 16 permitted characters is marked UPS-shaped, not a confirmed shipment.

Supported inputs and measurement

tracking/reference text. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

Formats overlap among carriers; a possible match is not verification, and no personal tracking data leave the browser.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
