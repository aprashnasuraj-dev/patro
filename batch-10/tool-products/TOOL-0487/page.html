What it does

Compute raised-bed fill volume, topsoil/compost proportions, bag count, and ordering buffer in metric or imperial inputs.

How to use

  1. Choose the task-specific settings and provide Dimension unit, Inside bed length, Inside bed width.
  2. Press the calculation action and check outputs, assumptions and any warnings before relying on the figures.
  3. Copy the explanation or export the local JSON estimate when a record is needed.

Worked example

Two 8×4×1 ft raised beds need 64 ft³ before allowance; at 35% compost, ~22.4 ft³ compost before settling.

Supported inputs and measurement

numeric quantities · option selectors. Each numeric field is bounded and finite. Units stated beside inputs remain the source of truth; conversion only occurs when explicitly indicated.

Assumptions and limitations

Volumes assume fully rectangular uniform depth, settled geometry and user-entered compost ratio; monitor soil tests, phosphorus and salts. Bulk yards and bags vary by compaction/settling.

FAQ: is this an official determination?

No. This is a transparent local estimate, classification, or screening. The primary standards, governing local rules, product documents and measured facts control authoritative decisions.
