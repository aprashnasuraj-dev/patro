Golden Hour and Sun Position Calculator

Calculate geometric solar elevation/azimuth and user-defined golden-hour elevation crossings for a civil date and location, with explicit polar-day/night handling.

Result

Use the sample state or enter your values, then run the tool.

What this tool does

It computes geometric sun position from UTC instants and finds when the Sun crosses two user-defined elevation thresholds. This avoids pretending “golden hour” has one universal official definition.

How to use it

Enter a civil date, latitude/longitude, IANA time zone and elevation bounds. The result shows morning/evening threshold crossings plus the day’s sampled maximum solar elevation.

Worked example

With defaults −4° and +6°, a normal mid-latitude day has four crossings: lower/upper while rising, then upper/lower while setting.

Supported formats

Gregorian date, latitude strictly between −90° and +90°, longitude −180° to +180°, IANA time-zone ID and elevation thresholds −18° to +30°.

Assumptions

Solar geometry follows NOAA-style equations. Refraction, terrain/horizon obstruction, elevation above sea level and weather are not modeled.

Limitations

Crossing times are numerical approximations and “golden hour” is an artistic convention, not an astronomical standard. Near the poles some or all threshold crossings may not occur.

Privacy and security

Normal use is browser-local. Inputs are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party by this tool.
