Business Card Maker with Print-Ready PDF and Bleed

Create a vector business-card PDF with selectable trim preset, configurable bleed, crop marks and plain-text contact layout entirely in the browser; explicitly reports commercial-print limitations.

Result

Use the sample state or enter your values, then run the tool.

What this tool does

It creates a physical-size PDF page equal to trim plus bleed on every side, draws the design into bleed, and adds simple crop marks around the trim box.

How to use it

Choose a regional trim preset and bleed, enter card text/colors, generate, then send the PDF only after checking the printer’s specifications.

Worked example

US 88.9×50.8 mm with 3 mm bleed yields a 94.9×56.8 mm PDF page.

Supported formats

US, EU and Japan trim presets; configurable 0–10 mm bleed; vector text PDF.

Assumptions

Default Helvetica is sufficient and RGB output is acceptable for a draft/office print workflow.

Limitations

Not PDF/X and not color-managed for commercial press. It does not embed logos/images or custom fonts in this source package. Printer bleed/crop-mark requirements vary.

Privacy and security

Normal use is browser-local. Inputs are not sent to a worker, account service, or third party by this tool.
