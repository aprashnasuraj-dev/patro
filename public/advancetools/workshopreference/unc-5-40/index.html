How the calculation works

60° thread: pitch diameter ≈ D − 0.649519P; external minor ≈ D − 1.226869P; stress area ≈ π/4 × (D − 0.9382P)²

Worked example

#5 – 40 UNC: D − P = 3.175 − 0.635 = 2.54 mm.

Before you use the result

Idealized profile geometry only. These values omit tolerance classes, coatings, root variations, tool compensation and material effects. Nominal thread percentage is a conventional estimate; the tool maker may prescribe a different pre-drill. Clearance and axial engagement are user-selected, not standards-certified.

Can I change the inputs?

Yes. Enter your own measurements and calculate again. A saved calculation sheet records the values used; it does not update when you change this page.
