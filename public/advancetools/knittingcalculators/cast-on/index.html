How the calculation works

width × gauge ÷ 10; nearest repeat + edges

Worked example

A 40 cm panel at 20 stitches per 10 cm needs 80 stitches before repeat adjustment.

Before you use the result

Measure a washed swatch in the actual stitch. Border stitches count toward the reported width.

Can I change the inputs?

Yes. Enter your own measurements and calculate again. A saved calculation sheet records the values used; it does not update when you change this page.

Lengths use centimetres unless labelled otherwise. Yarn lengths use metres. Estimates are based on your inputs, not a finished knitting pattern.
