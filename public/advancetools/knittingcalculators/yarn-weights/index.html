Yarn weight categories explained

Yarn weight means thickness category, not how many grams a skein weighs. Two yarns in the same category can have different fibre, twist, drape and gauge.

CategoryCommon namePlanning note
0LaceVery fine fabric; verify actual yarn length and gauge.
1Super fineIncludes many sock and fingering yarns.
2FineOften called sport or baby yarn.
3LightOften called DK or light worsted.
4MediumIncludes many worsted and aran yarns.
5BulkyThicker stitches; measure a blocked sample.
6Super bulkyLarge stitch dimensions can magnify rounding error.
7JumboVery large stitches; label and hand tension matter.
Category names help compare labels but do not guarantee substitution. Use a measured swatch and the yarn-substitution calculator.
