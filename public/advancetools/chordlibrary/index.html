How this library finds shapes

For each open-string tuning, a music-theory engine searches frets 0–12 for all the required chord tones. It excludes non-chord tones and keeps a compact fret span. Muted courses and open courses are shown explicitly.

A theoretically correct voicing still needs a practical fingering. Hand size, barre technique and the instrument setup affect playability. Audio is a synthesized pitch preview, not an instrument recording; doubled courses are represented by one pitch.
