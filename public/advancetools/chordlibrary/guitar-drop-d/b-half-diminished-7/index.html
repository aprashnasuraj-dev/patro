Reading these B half-diminished seventh shapes

Courses run left to right in this tuning: D2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4. A circle above the nut means open; a cross means mute. Numbers inside the dots are fret numbers, not finger numbers. A fret label at the side marks shapes higher up the neck.

Six strings in DADGBE; only the lowest string changes from standard guitar.

Why these notes?

Chord tones: B, D, F, A. Intervals from the root: 0, 3, 6, 10 semitones. Chord tone spellings follow the chord degrees; sounding-pitch labels may use an enharmonic equivalent.

Does every shape use the root in the bass?

No. Complete inversions are included, with root-bass shapes preferred when otherwise similar. In re-entrant tuning, the leftmost course may not be the lowest sounding pitch.

Shapes are ranked by fret height, fret span, muting and bass note. Check barre feasibility before playing. The synthesized preview represents pitches only, without doubled or octave-paired strings.
