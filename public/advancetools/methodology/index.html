Original calculation and drawing engines

Knitting formulas use measured gauge, geometric fabric area and entered yarn labels. Yarn category and tool size labels are references, not interchangeable yarn recommendations. Craft Yarn Council: sizing conventions; swatch and substitution guidance.

Chord shapes are calculated from semitone intervals and the explicitly listed tunings. The shapes contain every required pitch class; physical fingering is not certified. Audio is synthesized rather than recorded.

Workshop values use idealized 60-degree thread geometry. Cut-tap drill estimates differ from forming-tap pre-drills. We do not reproduce ISO/ANSI tolerance tables or claim certified fit classes. ISO 965 tolerance-system scope; Sandvik Coromant: cutting versus forming taps.

Paper drawings are original vector geometry. PDF uses the Helvetica base font for labels; paper patterns do not embed proprietary font files.

Privacy and accuracy

These tools do not upload inputs or save pasted text. Downloads are made on your device. The parent website may have its own privacy policy and analytics; consult that policy separately.

Retain original documents and check consequential results. A correct formula can still give an unsuitable result when the input, model or assumed encoding is wrong.
