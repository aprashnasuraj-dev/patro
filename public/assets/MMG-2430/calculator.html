Camera pixel scale and field-of-view calculator

Browser-only geometric estimate. Original detector dimensions are physical pixels, not already-binned or resampled output pixels. Nothing is uploaded.

Limit: Nominal small-angle geometry only. This does not solve a WCS or estimate real astrometric accuracy. Reducer factor assumed exact; field distortion and cropping not modeled.

Equation: scale (arcsec/physical pixel) = (180×3600/π÷1000) × pitch(µm) ÷ [focal length(mm) × multiplier]. All calculations occur in this page's JavaScript.
