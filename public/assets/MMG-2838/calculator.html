Binomial confidence interval explorer

Enter independent successes and trials. Compare Wilson, conservative exact binomial and ordinary Wald intervals. All displayed intervals are two-sided.

Enter values and select Calculate interval.

Assumptions: a fixed number of independent Bernoulli trials with a common success probability. Confidence is repeated-sampling coverage, not a posterior probability. Inputs remain on your device. This draft asset is not published.
