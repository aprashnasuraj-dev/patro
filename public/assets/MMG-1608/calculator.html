Model and limits

a(t)=A sin(2πft), v(t)=−A cos(2πft)/(2πf), u(t)=−A sin(2πft)/(2πf)². PGA is A; PGV is A/(2πf). Zero-mean periodic integration constants are assumed. Real records require baseline correction, instrument metadata and frequency content. Neither the calculator nor its CSV is a code-compliant structural analysis.
