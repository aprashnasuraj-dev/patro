# Editorial standard — AafnaiPatro Editor Team
Write genuinely useful English for an international audience. Distinct formats: article, blog, fact, answer. Answer the query clearly early, use meaningful headings, concrete comparisons, limitations and original analysis. No false first-hand claims or keyword padding. At least 5 and at most 20 distinct independently vetted, actually cited sources, trace each claim and provide author–date APA 7 style references with author, year/date, title, publisher/periodical, DOI or canonical URL; mark incomplete metadata for editorial review. Use at least one legally cleared image/diagram/chart with descriptive alt text and credit. Video and audio only if relevant, embeddable, licensed and accessible, with captions/transcripts where available; no autoplay. Reject unsupported statistics, unverified quotes and unsupported media. Editor approval is independent of a numeric score.
