# Code the calendars, clear the name-day rights

Three of the four page families are calculation problems, and the research has already solved them: the Ethiopian calendar, the Balinese Pawukon with Javanese weton, and the moon-sign calendars can all be computed exactly. Each algorithm below has been checked against independent references. The Ethiopian converter matched ICU/CLDR on all 80,353 days from 1890 to 2109, and the Pawukon code matched every published 2026 Balinese holiday. The fourth family, European name days, is a data-rights problem. No country publishes a freely licensed list. Only Latvia (state gazette decisions) and Slovakia (a Ministry of Culture advisory list) have state-issued sources. Everywhere else the clean options are Wikidata (CC0) or Wikipedia (CC BY-SA, share-alike). Finland is off-limits, because the University of Helsinki holds copyright. On competition, Ethiopian "today/converter" queries are currently held by parasite-SEO doorway pages that contain no working tool. Indonesian weton and Bali queries are held by daily news articles rather than persistent tools. Spanish and Italian moon-gardening results are mostly static annual tables. Strong incumbents dominate French, Spanish and Greek name days and German moon "heute" pages. The recommended order is: Ethiopian first, then weton/Pawukon, then moon calendars (Spanish and Italian gardening plus German haircuts), and name days last. Name days should start with Slovakia, Latvia, Hungary and Czechia, with Poland added once its data is rebuilt from a licensed source. One caveat applies to every family: no Google SERP positions or keyword volumes could be measured, so every competition judgement comes from site inspection, not ranking data.

## The Ethiopian calendar is fully computable, and its search results are mostly spam

### A one-line epoch reproduces ICU for 220 years

The Ethiopian year has twelve 30-day months plus Pagume, which has 5 days or 6 in a leap year. Year Y is a leap year when **Y mod 4 = 3, with no exceptions**, and the epoch is Julian 29 August 8 CE ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_calendar)). Expressed as a Julian Day Number, 1 Meskerem 1 E.C. is **JDN 1724221**. That equals ICU's `JD_EPOCH_OFFSET_AMETE_MIHRET = 1723856` once you add 365 for ICU's year-zero counting ([ICU ethpccal.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/unicode-org/icu/main/icu4c/source/i18n/ethpccal.cpp)). The Amete Alem era is the Amete Mihret year plus 5500 ([EOTC Medhane Alem](https://www.eotc-ma.com/post/bahire-hasab-the-ethiopian-calendar)). The researchers ran the routine below against Node 22's ICU 77.1/CLDR 47 `ethiopic` calendar for every day from 1 January 1890 to 31 December 2109 and found **zero mismatches**:

```
EPOCH = 1724221
eth_to_jdn(y,m,d) = EPOCH + 365*(y-1) + floor(y/4) + 30*(m-1) + (d-1)      # m = 1..13
jdn_to_eth(j):  y = floor((4*(j-EPOCH) + 1463) / 1461)
                m = floor((j - eth_to_jdn(y,1,1)) / 30) + 1
                d = j - eth_to_jdn(y,m,1) + 1
is_leap(y)     = (y mod 4 == 3)        # Pagume 6
weekday(j)     = j mod 7               # 0 = Monday ... 6 = Sunday
Gregorian→JDN (Fliegel–Van Flandern): a=(14-m)//12; Y=y+4800-a; M=m+12a-3
                JDN = d + (153M+2)//5 + 365Y + Y//4 - Y//100 + Y//400 - 32045
```

In JavaScript the floor divisions must use `Math.floor`, not `|0`. Do not hard-code "New Year = 11 or 12 September". That pattern holds only from March 1900 to February 2100. 2100 is an Ethiopian leap year but not a Gregorian one, so after it every Gregorian equivalent shifts a day later ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopian_calendar)). The same shift moves fixed Ethiopian holidays around Gregorian leap years: Meskel falls on 28 September in 2027 and 2031, and Timkat on 20 January in 2028.

### Bahire Hasab yields Orthodox Easter and doubles as the Greek and Bulgarian name-day engine

The Ethiopian computus was rebuilt from the EOTC Medhane Alem description. That description contains typos: a mislabelled year heading, a wrong remainder, and a weekly-fast offset of 119 that should be 121. It also says the Tewsak "always falls on Tuesday", which contradicts its own table. **Do not copy it verbatim** ([EOTC-MA](https://www.eotc-ma.com/post/bahire-hasab-the-ethiopian-calendar)). Corrected, the steps are as follows. The Evangelist is AA mod 4, with 1 Matewos, 2 Markos, 3 Lukas and 0 Yohannes, and the Lukas year is the leap year. Medeb is AA mod 19, and Wenber is Medeb − 1 (or 18 when Medeb is 0). Metqe is (19 × Wenber) mod 30, and Beale Metqe falls on Meskerem *Metqe* if Metqe > 14, otherwise on Tikimt *Metqe*. Tewsak is set by the weekday of Beale Metqe: Sun 7, Mon 6, Tue 5, Wed 4, Thu 3, Fri 2, Sat 8. Nenewe (always a Monday) is Beale Metqe + 120 + Tewsak. **Fasika is Nenewe + 69**. The other movable observances are offsets from Nenewe: Abiy Tsom +14, Hosanna +62, Siklet +67, Erget +108 and Paraclete +118. Implemented this way, it equals Julian-computus Orthodox Easter for every year from 1600 to 2399. Fasika for 2025–2030 and 2032 matches Wikipedia's Eastern Easter table: **20 Apr 2025, 12 Apr 2026, 2 May 2027, 16 Apr 2028, 8 Apr 2029, 28 Apr 2030, 2 May 2032**, and the computed 13 Apr 2031 ([Wikipedia: List of dates for Easter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_dates_for_Easter)). Metqe never equals 14, so the source's "= 14" branch never fires. Metqe 0 should be treated as Meskerem 30.

The result serves more than one family. The same Easter date drives every movable Greek and Bulgarian name day (see the name-day section). One computus module therefore covers both families and is tested once.

### Display rules ICU will not give you

CLDR 47 has no Amharic era name. ICU prints "ቅዳሜ፣ 30 መስከረም 2019 ERA1", so the page must append **ዓ.ም.** itself. ICU also prints Latin digits even with `-u-nu-ethi`, so Ge'ez numerals (U+1369–U+137C, which have no zero) must be generated manually; for example, 2019 is ፳፻፲፱. ICU spells month 13 **ጳጉሜን**. Ethiopian clock time also has to be built separately. The day count starts at about 06:00, so "8 am is 2 o'clock … 6 pm … is 12 o'clock" ([UPenn African Studies](https://www.africa.upenn.edu/eue_web/time.htm)). The formula is h_E = ((h_W + 5) mod 12) + 1, and ICU's Amharic time format still uses Western hours. Public holidays come in three kinds. Seven are fixed in the Ethiopian calendar: Genna, Timkat, Adwa, Patriots' Day, Derg Downfall, Enkutatash and Meskel. Labour Day is fixed in the Gregorian calendar. The rest are movable: Siklet and Fasika by Bahire Hasab, and three Hijri holidays that timeanddate marks as tentative ([timeanddate 2026](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/ethiopia/2026)). Store the fixed holidays as Ethiopian dates and convert them for display, rather than storing Gregorian dates.

### Doorway pages hold the results; a working tool has no real content rival

The test searches for "Ethiopian calendar today", "ethiopian calendar converter" and Amharic "የኢትዮጵያ ቀን መቁጠሪያ ዛሬ" all returned keyword-stuffed doorway pages on hijacked institutional subdomains. Examples were chw.dhis2.org, lbas.lboro.ac.uk, blog.mozilla.com.tw and iris.londoncouncils.gov.uk. The top "converter" result has **no input fields, no output and no script** ([chw.dhis2.org](https://chw.dhis2.org/calendar/ethiopian-calendar-converter.html)). No genuine Amharic-language tool appeared. The real competitors were apps and libraries ([Geez Calendar app](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id6754090442), [ethiopian-date-converter-py](https://pypi.org/project/ethiopian-date-converter-py/)) and holiday aggregators such as timeanddate. This is the weakest content competition of all four families. Google's site-reputation-abuse policy could also clear the spam network without warning. The research tool was not Google, however, so established converter sites may rank there and simply not have appeared.

## Pawukon and weton share one 210-day engine; the Balinese lunar calendar must come from published tables

### Reingold–Dershowitz formulas, verified against ten published references

The Pawukon algorithm comes from the Reingold & Dershowitz *Calendrical Calculations* source code, Apache-2.0 ([calendar.l](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EdReingold/calendar-code2/main/calendar.l)). Its epoch, R.D. −1,721,279, is confirmed by the Elixir `calendars` library ([hexdocs BaliPawukon](https://calendars.hexdocs.pm/BaliPawukon.html)). Using the integer JDN of the local civil date:

```
day        = (JDN - 146) mod 210            # 0 = Redite Paing Sinta (Banyu Pinaruh)
wuku       = 1 + floor(day/7)                # 1..30, Sinta..Watugunung
saptawara  = 1 + day mod 7                   # 1 Redite..7 Saniscara; urip S=[5,4,3,7,8,6,9]
pancawara  = amod(day+2, 5)                  # 1 Umanis,2 Paing,3 Pon,4 Wage,5 Kliwon; urip P=[5,9,7,4,8]
sadwara    = 1 + day mod 6                   # Tungleh..Maulu
triwara    = 1 + day mod 3                   # Pasah, Beteng, Kajeng (no anomaly)
astawara   = 1 + (max(6, 4 + ((day-70) mod 210)) mod 8)   # Kala x3 on days 70-72
caturwara  = amod(astawara, 4)               # Jaya x3 on days 70-72
sangawara  = 1 + (max(0, day-3) mod 9)       # Dangu x4 on days 0-3
dasawara   = (1 + P[panca-1] + S[sapta-1]) mod 10   # 0 Pandita..9 Raksasa
dwiwara    = amod(dasawara, 2)               # 1 Menga, 2 Pepet
luang      = (dasawara is even)
pasaran    = ["Legi","Pahing","Pon","Wage","Kliwon"][(day+1) mod 5]
```

Only caturwara, astawara and sangawara are irregular, as Wikipedia describes ([Pawukon calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pawukon_calendar)). The "triwara adjustment" some briefs mention does not exist in the authoritative algorithm. Test vectors, each re-run while writing this report: **2001-09-11** is day 58, Anggara Kliwon Julungwangi, with the full ten-wara vector (Luang, Pepet, Beteng, Jaya, Kliwon, Was, Anggara, Guru, Jangur, Suka) as in the [hexdocs test vector](https://calendars.hexdocs.pm/BaliPawukon.html). **2022-10-23** is day 0, Redite Paing Sinta ([detikBali](https://www.detik.com/bali/bali-bungah/d-6361787/kalender-bali-23-oktober-2022-redite-paing-sinta-banyu-pinaruh)). **2026-06-17** is day 73, Buda Kliwon Dungulan, which is Galungan ([Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/lifestyle/varia/6a3244b6b5cf1/kapan-hari-raya-galungan-dan-kuningan-2026-simak-jadwal-dan-perayaannya)). **2026-10-10** is day 188, Saniscara Kliwon Wayang, which is Tumpek Wayang ([detikBali 2026](https://www.detik.com/bali/berita/d-8293016/jadwal-lengkap-hari-raya-hindu-sepanjang-tahun-2026-menurut-kalender-bali)). **17 Aug 1945** is Jumat Legi, matching Soekarno's remark "Hari Jumat ini Jumat Legi" ([GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2021/08/17/makna-weton-hingga-kisah-mistik-soekarno-pilih-proklamasi-pada-17-agustus)). The July 2026 Kajeng Kliwon and Anggar Kasih days also match Liputan6's monthly calendar ([Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/hot/read/8120280/kalender-bali-bulan-juli-2026)). Kabar24's June 2023 Javanese calendar confirms that Javanese wuku and pasaran are synchronized with the Balinese cycle ([Kabar24](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20230511/243/1655145/kalender-jawa-juni-2023-lengkap-pasaran-dan-wuku)).

All Pawukon holidays are fixed day indices, so they recur every 210 days. Banyu Pinaruh is 0, Pagerwesi 3, Galungan 73, Kuningan 83 and Saraswati 209, and Tumpek falls on days 13 + 35k. The computed 2026 dates match detikBali's full list. Computed 2027 dates have **no published confirmation yet**: Galungan 13 Jan and 11 Aug, Kuningan 23 Jan and 21 Aug, Saraswati 29 May and 25 Dec. Otonan is simply birth + 210n, since every wewaran repeats on that cycle ([BASAbali Wiki](https://basaibubali.org/Holiday_or_Ceremony_Upacara_Aoton?setlang=id)).

### Weton jodoh is a mod-8 lookup; the Javanese month needs more work

The neptu values are Minggu 5, Senin 4, Selasa 3, Rabu 7, Kamis 8, Jumat 6, Sabtu 9 for the weekdays and Legi 5, Pahing 9, Pon 7, Wage 4, Kliwon 8 for the pasaran. They are identical to the Balinese urip arrays. Weton jodoh is read from (sum of both partners' neptu − 1) mod 8, giving 0 Pegat, 1 Ratu, 2 Jodoh, 3 Topo, 4 Tinari, 5 Padu, 6 Sujanan, 7 Pesthi ([detikJateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/budaya/d-6608082/tata-cara-hitungan-weton-jawa-untuk-pernikahan-makna-contoh-dan-ramalan/amp)). The source prints "23" under Pegat, an evident typo for 33. Its secondary seven-outcome "ramalan" method is internally inconsistent, and other pancasuda labels differ ([kalenderku](https://kalenderku.id/21-oktober-2022)), so **ship only the 8-step table** at launch. The Javanese (Sultan Agung) lunar date is a separate tabular calendar with 8-year windu and 120-year kurup cycles; the current kurup, Alip Selasa Pon, runs to 25 Aug 2052 ([Wikipedia: Javanese calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_calendar)). It can run a day ahead of the government Hijri date: 30 Jul 2025 was "4 Sapar 1959" but "5 Safar 1447 H" ([Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/regional/read/6117832/kalender-jawa-dan-wetonnya-hari-ini-rabu-30-juli-2025)). 1 Suro 1960 fell on Rabu Kliwon, 17 Jun 2026, and malam 1 Suro was the evening before ([detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/budaya/d-8517446/malam-1-suro-2026-bertepatan-weton-apa)). The exact Asapon year-length placement was not verified beyond Wikipedia, so test the Javanese month display against these anchors before launch.

### Sasih, purnama and tilem resist calculation

The Balinese Saka calendar is lunisolar. Its full and new moons follow the traditional pangalantaka arithmetic, a 63-tithi correction per 64 tithi re-anchored over time. The anchor was moved by a PHDI decision in 1998, and the source admits "Di Bali tidak dikenal, kapan pastinya Pangalantaka digeser" ([Pangalantaka PDF](https://purikauhanubud.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/PANGALANTAKA2.pdf)). The rules for the intercalary month also conflict. Wikipedia places it after month 11 or 12 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balinese_saka_calendar)), while BASAbali says seven months can take a *nampih* and that the naming changed in 1993 ([BASAbali](https://basaibubali.org/Nampih)). The decisive evidence is **Nyepi 2027: Monday 8 March under the SKB 3 Menteri** signed 15 Sep 2026 ([Setneg](https://setneg.go.id/baca/index/inilah_skb_3_menteri_libur_nasional_dan_cuti_bersama_2027)), against 9 March on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyepi). The Saka layer must therefore be a curated data table built from the printed Kalender Bali, PHDI and the SKB. The 2026 purnama and tilem dates are published by detikBali, and years beyond the published decrees should be marked "provisional". Galungan is not a national holiday in the SKB list, so do not label it "libur nasional".

### Competition is article-heavy, and no one offers an otonan calculator

"hitung weton", "weton hari ini" and "kalender bali" return detik, Kabar24, GNFI, Liputan6, Tirto, Mamikos and Pegadaian articles, plus app roundups and at least one hijacked-subdomain spam page ([example](https://partnerwithscphealth-jitsi.bluetext.com/blog/weton-hari-ini-2025-your)). The one strong tool is **kalenderku.id**, which offers per-date pages with weton, neptu, wuku, Javanese date, windu, pancasuda and Hijri. It has **no Balinese sasih and no otonan** ([kalenderku](https://kalenderku.id/21-oktober-2022)). "otonan" queries return only journals and expat explainers such as [NOW! Bali](https://www.nowbali.co.id/otonan-birthday-celebrations-balinese-way/). "wuku hari ini" is polluted by Hajj "wukuf" results, so page copy should disambiguate.

### Frame divination as heritage and ceremonies as the priest's call

Islamic scholarship summarised by detikJatim, citing NU Online, treats weton as *mubah* when practised as custom, but *haram* if one believes its result is certain or decides fate ([detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/budaya/d-7228381/hukum-perhitungan-weton-menurut-islam)). Weton jodoh output should say "menurut tradisi primbon Jawa", carry a no-fate disclaimer, and let users hide labels like "Pegat". Avoid fear copy such as "weton sial". Otonan and Galungan are Balinese Hindu rites led by family elders or a pemangku, and practice varies by village ([NOW! Bali](https://www.nowbali.co.id/otonan-birthday-celebrations-balinese-way/)). Pages should send users to the family, a priest or the official Kalender Bali for ceremony timing, and should keep ads away from holy-day content. Two day-boundary issues need UI toggles. The Balinese day turns at sunrise ([Pangalantaka PDF](https://purikauhanubud.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/PANGALANTAKA2.pdf)), and the Javanese day at sunset. A birth between midnight and sunrise is therefore ambiguous for otonan, and a weton calculator needs a "lahir setelah maghrib" option.

## Moon calendars depend on choosing the right zodiac, which most competitors never state

### Two zodiacs, four cycles, one engine already in the repo

Every tradition combines four cycles. The first is the phase (waxing or waning). The second is the ascending or descending moon, meaning the trend of the moon's **declination**, which is a ~27.3-day cycle and *not* the node cycle ([iCalendrier](https://icalendrier.fr/lune/jardinage)). The third is the moon's sign, mapped to elements: Fire is fruit, Earth root, Air flower and Water leaf. The fourth is a set of "unfavourable" moments: nodes, perigee, apogee and eclipses, plus new-moon rest days on some German sites ([garteln.com](https://www.garteln.com/mondkalender/)). The zodiac choice splits the market. Paungger/Poppe compute signs from the vernal point, which in effect is the **tropical** zodiac, and they admit that the real lunar position can be a sign off ([Paungger/Poppe Leseprobe](https://prestelpublishing.penguinrandomhouse.de/leseprobe/Vom-richtigen-Zeitpunkt/leseprobe_9783453603554.pdf)). The Thun/biodynamic model uses the visible **constellations, with unequal widths** ([Thun-Verlag](https://thun-verlag.com/aussaattage2023/)). French and Spanish garden calendars mostly follow the constellation model.

The moon research computed October 2026 with `astronomy-engine` 2.1.19, which the project already uses in `src/patro-tools/core/astro.ts`. Constellation ingresses from `Astronomy.Constellation()` on J2000 RA/Dec fell on the transition days that icalendario.net and Gerbeaud publish ([icalendario](https://icalendario.net/calendario-jardineria-lunar-2026/), [Gerbeaud](https://www.gerbeaud.com/jardin/calendrier/calendrier-lunaire.php)). The match was near day-for-day, except for a small Pisces offset on 23 October. **Equal-30° Lahiri sidereal signs, the project's Hindu default, did not match.** For example, they put Libra on 11 October where the published calendars show root. The garden pages must therefore not reuse the existing ayanamsa code. The validation set below can become a CI test, with tolerances of ±15 min for phases and apsides, ±30 min for nodes and declination extrema, and an exact day-type per calendar day.

| Event, Oct 2026 (Berlin) | Computed | Published match |
|---|---|---|
| Perigee | 1 Oct 22:52 | icalendario 1 Oct |
| Descending node | 7 Oct 03:20 | icalendario, Gerbeaud 7 Oct |
| New moon | 10 Oct 17:50 | mondinfo.de 17:50 |
| Declination minimum (ascending starts) | 16 Oct 04:40 | Gerbeaud ascending from 16 Oct |
| Apogee | 17 Oct 00:54 | 17 Oct |
| Ascending node | 21 Oct 11:00 | 21 Oct |
| Full moon | 26 Oct 05:12 CET | mondinfo.de 05:12 |
| Perigee | 28 Oct 19:04 | 28 Oct |
| Declination maximum (descending starts) | 29 Oct 18:30 | Gerbeaud descending from 30 Oct |

Three issues remain open. The Ophiuchus stretch has to be assigned to Scorpio or Sagittarius; the published calendars show leaf on 15 Oct and fruit on 16 Oct, which implies most of it goes to Scorpio. A rule is needed for days with two day-types. And the computed tropical ingresses run **1–2 hours later** than mondkalender-online.de's, which is still unexplained ([mondkalender-online.de](https://www.mondkalender-online.de/mondkalender/empfehlungen/termine-der-empfehlung.php?ereignisid=160&metatitle=Haare+schneiden+%2F+waschen)). Check against a second tropical source such as paungger-poppe.com or Swiss Ephemeris before publishing times.

### Haircut rules are settled only for German

The mainstream German rule set is consistent across publishers. Cut in the waxing moon for growth and volume, and in the waning moon for short cuts that should hold their shape. Leo is best, Virgo good, and Pisces and Cancer should be avoided. The rules are attributed to Paungger-Poppe's "Tiroler Bergbauerntraditionen" ([ganz-wien.at](https://www.ganz-wien.at/lifestyle/schoenheit/haare-naegel/mondkalender-haare-schneiden.html)). mondinfo.de's favourable October dates line up with fire signs in *tropical* terms ([mondinfo.de](https://mondinfo.de/haarschnitt)), which confirms that haircut pages should use the tropical zodiac and garden pages the constellations. Scorpio advice conflicts even within one article ([t-online](https://www.t-online.de/leben/mode-beauty/id_67098780/wie-sie-sich-nach-dem-mondkalender-die-haare-schneiden.html)), so present it as a variant or leave it out. French has a scored daily hair tool ([calendrier-lunaire.net](https://www.calendrier-lunaire.net/se-coiffer-avec-la-lune)). **Spanish and Italian haircut rules were not researched**, so the ES and IT haircut pages are not ready to build. Italian gardening content is largely phase-only *lunario* advice, and even its leading guide says constellation days need "un calendario apposito" ([Orto Da Coltivare](https://www.ortodacoltivare.it/calendario/fasi-lunari/)). Italian pages therefore need a traditional phase-only mode alongside the biodynamic mode.

### German and French are crowded; Spanish and Italian have a gap

German has real interactive daily tools: mondkalender-online.de, mondinfo.de and paungger-poppe.com's "Der Mond heute" ([paungger-poppe.com](https://www.paungger-poppe.com/)). It also has daily "Mondkalender heute" articles from news sites ([Deavita](https://deavita.com/mondkalender-heute/mondkalender-9-oktober-rikta-tag-waage.html), [Ruhr Nachrichten](https://ruhrnachrichten.de/service/mondkalender-2026-oktober-neumond-vollmond-termine-wann-deutschland-w837025-2001087534)). That signals strong recurring demand and also strong competition. French has the strongest daily tools, iCalendrier and calendrier-lunaire.net, alongside large garden media. Spanish and Italian results are mostly annual tables, PDFs and phase-only pages, for example [icalendario.net](https://icalendario.net/calendario-jardineria-lunar-2026/), [Calendarr](https://www.calendarr.com/espana/calendario-lunar-2026/) and [idealista](https://www.idealista.it/news/finanza/casa/2026/07/11/403930-il-calendario-lunare-della-semina-2026-che-cambia-raccolti-e-orto). A per-day page with accurate ingress times and a stated zodiac system fills a visible gap there.

### Brand risk and science framing

"Maria Thun®" is printed with the registered mark by the publisher and by retailers ([Thun-Verlag](https://thun-verlag.com/aussaattage2023/), [LUBA](https://luba.de/en/?p=9638)). Do not use the name in titles, URLs or schema. Write "nach biodynamischer Tradition (Sternbild-Modell)" or the local equivalent instead. The register entries themselves (DPMA/EUIPO) were not checked, and neither was the status of "Aussaattage". Compute everything independently, because the published tables are "Alle Rechte vorbehalten". Every page needs a short, sourced disclaimer. A 2020 peer-reviewed *Agronomy* review found "no reliable, science-based evidence for any relationship between lunar phases and plant physiology" ([Mayoral et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy10070955)), and extension horticulturists say the same ([Garden Professors](https://gardenprofessors.com/lunar-control-or-lunacy), [NCSU Extension](https://henderson.ces.ncsu.edu/2025/02/gardening-by-signs-superstition-or-wisdom)). Leading competitors already carry such disclaimers ([iCalendrier](https://icalendrier.fr/lune/jardinage), [Orto Da Coltivare](https://www.ortodacoltivare.it/calendario/fasi-lunari/)), so adding one costs nothing in rankings.

## Name days hinge on licensing, and only a handful of countries have clean data

### Facts are free, compilations are not

A name-to-date pair is a fact. The CJEU's 2004 rulings held that the EU database right protects investment in *obtaining* existing data, not in *creating* it. Football fixture lists were held unprotected on that basis ([CJEU press release 89/04](https://curia.europa.eu/en/actu/communiques/cp04/aff/cp040089en.pdf)). That logic weakens a calendar publisher's claim over names it simply assigned to dates. It does not license wholesale scraping of a curated site. Nominis explicitly states "Toute reproduction, même partielle, est interdite" ([Nominis mentions légales](https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/mentions-legales.html)). Its JSON API asks only for a backlink, but that request is not a licence ([Nominis intégrations](https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/integrations.html)). **Wikidata structured data is CC0** ([Wikidata:Licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing)), and property P1750 is "name day". Wikipedia list pages are CC BY-SA, which requires attribution plus share-alike on the adapted dataset; the researchers could not fetch the licence page, so confirm it before launch. Several MIT-labelled npm packages are Wikipedia-derived. `nevnap` (HU) says its names "are from Wikipedia", and `namedays-cs` (CZ) credits "Jmeniny v Česku" ([nevnap](https://github.com/nevadavid/nevnap), [namedays-cs](https://github.com/OzzyCzech/namedays-cs)). Their MIT label does not remove the CC BY-SA obligation. The multi-country abalin API publishes no data licence or terms ([nameday.abalin.net](https://nameday.abalin.net/)), so use it for cross-checks only. **Finland is closed**: the University of Helsinki states it holds copyright to the Finnish lists ([helsinki.fi](https://www.helsinki.fi/fi/uutiset/kulttuuri/uusia-nimia-kalenteriin-vuonna-2025)). Sweden's list has had no official status since 1972 ([Riksdagen KrU4](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/betankande/officiell-namnlangd-m-m_gh01kru4/html/)), but no licence was found for the 2001 Swedish Academy list.

### Cultural weight sets which countries are worth the effort

Name days rival or beat birthdays in most of Poland ([Culture.pl](https://culture.pl/en/article/imieniny-0)), in Slovak and Hungarian workplaces, in Latvian families, and in Greece, where they are an open-house celebration ([Wikipedia: Name day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Name_day)). They carry the same weight among older Italians, especially in the South, and in Austria. Czechs and Croats treat them as minor. Short greetings such as "Wszystkiego najlepszego!", "Veľa všetkého dobrého!" and "Χρόνια πολλά!" are sourced and are not copyrightable. Most other greetings (Czech, Hungarian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, German, Spanish) are background knowledge only and need native review. Rhymed wish poems on se.pl or tipptar are likely protected and must not be copied. Pages should not claim to be "official" except for Slovakia ("podľa kalendára MK SR") and Latvia. Elsewhere "traditional calendar" is the accurate wording.

## Conclusion

Most of the remaining risk is about dates, not code. Every algorithm the project needs now has a test vector, but four calendar facts remain unsettled: Genna in January 2028, Nyepi and the nampih placement beyond 2027, Thun's exact constellation boundaries, and a missing Ethiopian holiday in 2027. In each case an algorithm alone cannot fix the date, because the authority is a church, a ministry decree or a printed almanac. The sound design is computed defaults with an authority-override table and visible "provisional" flags. That same table structure fits the Slovak and Latvian name-day lists, which change by official decision. The modules also reuse each other. The Bahire Hasab module that gives Ethiopian Fasika also gives Greek and Bulgarian movable name days. The Pawukon engine covers both Bali and Java. The existing `astronomy-engine` dependency covers all four moon markets, provided the Lahiri sidereal default is kept out of the gardening pages.

The strategic split follows. The computable families can launch on engineering effort alone, against competitors that are mostly spam or daily articles, and they let the site prove accuracy and transparency. Name days should then follow, in the countries where the data's legal status is cleanest rather than where demand is largest. The single most valuable next step for all four families is the one this research could not do: pull real Google SERPs and keyword volumes per market, since every ranking judgement here is an informed inference.
