What this tool does

Show remaining straight-line driving and duty headroom under a selected baseline US property-carrier or EU/UK driving-rule scenario.

How to use

  1. Enter the scenario values shown in the form.
  2. Run the calculation and review the assumptions and warnings.
  3. Copy or export the self-contained estimate for your records.

Worked example

Start with this editable scenario: Regulation set: US, Driving already completed today: 6, Driving since qualifying rest/break: 3. Select Calculate to review an explicit breakdown and warnings—not an official legal or tax determination.

Supported input formats and units

numbers / choices, hours, hours, hours, hours. Dates use the Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD civil-date format. Only listed input ranges are supported.

Assumptions and limitations

Not a certified ELD/tachograph; user must verify rolling periods, breaks, rest and exceptions.

Frequently asked question

Does this result replace an official filing, legal determination or professional safety inspection?

No. The model is a transparent scenario estimate. Confirm your date, local rule, rates and applicable eligibility with a primary authority.
