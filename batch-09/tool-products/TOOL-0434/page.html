What this tool does

Translate driving needs into delivered and grid electricity, charging time and a continuous-load circuit comparison.

How to use

  1. Enter the scenario values shown in the form.
  2. Run the calculation and review the assumptions and warnings.
  3. Copy or export the self-contained estimate for your records.

Worked example

Start with this editable scenario: Travel miles per day: 40, Battery energy per 100 miles: 30, Charging efficiency estimate: 88. Select Calculate to review an explicit breakdown and warnings—not an official legal or tax determination.

Supported input formats and units

mi, kWh/100 mi, %, $/kWh, kW. Dates use the Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD civil-date format. Only listed input ranges are supported.

Assumptions and limitations

Electrical safety and applicable NEC sections vary by jurisdiction and installation. Rate does not include demand-based pricing unless user includes it.

Frequently asked question

Does this result replace an official filing, legal determination or professional safety inspection?

No. The model is a transparent scenario estimate. Confirm your date, local rule, rates and applicable eligibility with a primary authority.
