Dog & Cat Food Portion and Cost Calculator by Brand

What this tool calculates

Label-sourced feeding portions and bag cost. Compare feeding portions and per-day cost using the brand/product feeding energy data supplied from the actual package, not an unverified food database.

Instructions

  1. Select your scenario and enter measured or verified source values.
  2. Calculate to review results, assumptions and caveats.
  3. Copy the breakdown or export structured inputs and outputs locally.

Worked example

Use the editable sample inputs Species: dog, Brand and formula on food label: Example Adult Maintenance, Calories from package label: 360. The expected reference metric is Food servings per day: 1.35 cups. The final result remains contingent on every entered value and disclosed condition.

Supported formats and units

%, currency, g/cup, kcal/cup, kcal/day, kg, listed choices, short text. Values outside field bounds, non-finite numeric strings and impossible calendar dates are rejected.

Assumptions and limitations

Not a veterinary diet prescription; contraindications, disease-specific foods, species diet and individual energy needs are not evaluated.

FAQ: Can I use this output as an official approval or professional prescription?

No. Calculations are local illustrations subject to current authorities, equipment specifications, product labeling and professional evaluation where applicable.
