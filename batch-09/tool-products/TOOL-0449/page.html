Supported formats and units

ISO Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD, listed choices, optional Gregorian date. Values outside field bounds, non-finite numeric strings and impossible calendar dates are rejected.

Assumptions and limitations

Federal COBRA only; no state mini-COBRA, extraordinary extensions, receipt/mail nuances or plan-specific grace calculations.
