Supported formats and units

ISO YYYY-MM-DD, days, explicit choice, months, up to 100 characters, years. Numeric fields must be finite and within the displayed bounds. Lists and dates are checked for structure and plausible ranges.

Assumptions and known limitations

Rules differ by claim and jurisdiction. Even correct interval arithmetic cannot determine accrual or whether a weekend rule applies.

FAQ: Is the output authoritative?

The result is not professional certification; the source inputs and governing rules control the decision.
