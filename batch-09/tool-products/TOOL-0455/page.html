What this tool calculates

Dentist-approved clear aligner change calendar. Plan a user-specified aligner schedule by fixed days per tray; print a chronological transition list and avoid recommending changes to clinical wear requirements.

Instructions

  1. Select your scenario and enter measured or verified source values.
  2. Calculate to review results, assumptions and caveats.
  3. Copy the breakdown or export structured inputs and outputs locally.

Worked example

Use the editable sample inputs First tray start date: 2026-11-01, Total aligner trays: 18, Prescribed whole days per tray: 10. The expected reference metric is Total calendar-day interval: 180 days. The final result remains contingent on every entered value and disclosed condition.

Supported formats and units

ISO Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD, hours, numbers. Values outside field bounds, non-finite numeric strings and impossible calendar dates are rejected.

Assumptions and limitations

Not an orthodontic treatment recommendation. The generic interval is explicitly user-supplied and clinical suitability is not established.

FAQ: Can I use this output as an official approval or professional prescription?

No. Calculations are local illustrations subject to current authorities, equipment specifications, product labeling and professional evaluation where applicable.
