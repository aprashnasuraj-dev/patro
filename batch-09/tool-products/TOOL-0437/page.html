What this tool does

Allocate consumed fuel over jurisdictions by taxable miles and compare calculated gross fuel tax with verified per-jurisdiction tax-paid fuel credit.

How to use

  1. Enter the scenario values shown in the form.
  2. Run the calculation and review the assumptions and warnings.
  3. Copy or export the self-contained estimate for your records.

Worked example

Supported input formats and units

text, gal, structured JSON. Dates use the Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD civil-date format. Only listed input ranges are supported.

Assumptions and limitations

Not filing-ready; jurisdiction surcharge, credit limitations and historical quarter table must be reconciled.

Frequently asked question

Does this result replace an official filing, legal determination or professional safety inspection?

No. The model is a transparent scenario estimate. Confirm your date, local rule, rates and applicable eligibility with a primary authority.
