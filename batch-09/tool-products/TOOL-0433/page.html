Tongue Weight, Payload & GCWR Checker

Compare truck payload, hitch tongue capacity, tow rating and gross combined rating against a loaded trailer scenario.

What this tool does

Compare truck payload, hitch tongue capacity, tow rating and gross combined rating against a loaded trailer scenario.

How to use

  1. Enter the scenario values shown in the form.
  2. Run the calculation and review the assumptions and warnings.
  3. Copy or export the self-contained estimate for your records.

Worked example

Start with this editable scenario: Loaded trailer actual mass: 5000, Tongue percentage of trailer actual mass: 12, Available vehicle payload rating: 1600. Select Calculate to review an explicit breakdown and warnings—not an official legal or tax determination.

Supported input formats and units

lb, %, lb, lb, lb. Dates use the Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD civil-date format. Only listed input ranges are supported.

Assumptions and limitations

Vehicle GVWR, axle ratings, hitch configurations, manufacturer derating and sway/brakes not assessed.

Frequently asked question

Does this result replace an official filing, legal determination or professional safety inspection?

No. The model is a transparent scenario estimate. Confirm your date, local rule, rates and applicable eligibility with a primary authority.
