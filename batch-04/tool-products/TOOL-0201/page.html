A/B Test Significance Calculator with SRM Check

Compare two independent conversion rates with a pooled two-proportion z-test, confidence interval, uplift, and sample-ratio-mismatch check. Inputs and calculations stay in the browser during normal use.

Inputs

Use the sample values or enter your own data, then run the tool.

What this tool calculates

Compare two independent conversion rates with a pooled two-proportion z-test, confidence interval, uplift, and sample-ratio-mismatch check.

How to use it

Enter or paste the requested data, review the model choices shown beside the controls, run the calculation, then inspect warnings and method labels before copying or exporting the result.

Example

The form is preloaded with a bounded example so you can see the expected input shape before replacing it with your own data.

Assumptions

Two independent variants; conversions are binary; assignment proportion is known in advance.

Limitations

The z-test is an asymptotic approximation and assumes independent observations and stable random assignment. A significant SRM check is diagnostic, not proof of a specific instrumentation bug.

FAQ

Does a low p-value prove the treatment caused the change?

No. The test quantifies sampling evidence under the stated randomized-design assumptions; instrumentation, SRM, peeking, multiplicity, and implementation issues still matter.

Why include SRM?

A sample-ratio mismatch can reveal assignment or logging problems before uplift is interpreted.
