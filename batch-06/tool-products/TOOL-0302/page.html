Exact Date Difference Calculator (Years, Months, Days)

Decompose two Gregorian dates into calendar years, months and days using an explicit clamp-and-advance convention, while also reporting exact total days.

Result

Use the sample state or enter your values, then run the tool.

What this tool does

It reports both a human calendar-component decomposition and an unambiguous exact count of elapsed civil days.

How to use it

Enter any two valid Gregorian dates. The tool handles either order and tells you the decomposition convention rather than hiding month-end choices.

Worked example

31 January 2024 to 1 March 2024 is 30 total days. Under this tool’s clamp-and-advance convention it decomposes to 0 years, 1 month and 1 day.

Supported formats

Strict Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD dates from year 0001 through 9999.

Assumptions

Calendar components are computed by advancing whole years, then whole months without passing the target; invalid same-day month anchors clamp to month end.

Limitations

Years/months/days decompositions are convention-dependent around month ends. Use total days when a convention-independent duration is required.

FAQ

Does normal use transmit my data?

No. The production intent is browser-side processing with no account, worker call or third-party transmission.

How are ambiguous dates or times handled?

The tool exposes the relevant convention, calendar variant, time zone or clamping rule and returns an explicit warning or error when a silent guess would be misleading.
