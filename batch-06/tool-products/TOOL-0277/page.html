What this tool does

Estimate segmental retaining-wall blocks, buried courses, caps, drainage-zone aggregate and leveling-base volume while explicitly stopping short of structural wall design.

How to use it

Enter measured project values, review any product or code assumptions, then use the primary calculation action. Inputs are processed in the browser and bounded before calculation.

Worked example

For a 30 ft wall with 6 in courses and one buried course, the tool counts courses and blocks, then estimates a 12 in drainage zone and entered leveling-base section.

Supported formats and units

Inputs are explicitly labeled. Unit conversions use exact SI/customary conversion factors where applicable; product coverage values remain user-configurable.

Assumptions

Courses are rounded up from exposed height plus user-entered buried courses. Drainage aggregate is a simple rectangular zone behind the full modeled block height.

Limitations

This tool does not determine wall stability, geogrid, embedment, soil bearing, surcharge, groundwater or drainage-system capacity. Product manufacturer and engineered drawings control.

FAQ

Does this send project data to a server?

No during normal use. The production intent is browser-side execution with no account or upload.

Can this replace a code-compliant design or product takeoff?

No. Where structure, electrical safety, HVAC sizing, jurisdiction rules or manufacturer coverage matter, the tool exposes assumptions and warnings rather than claiming approval.
