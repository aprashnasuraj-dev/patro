Add or Subtract Days, Weeks, Months from a Date

Add or subtract whole days, weeks or calendar months from a Gregorian date with an explicit end-of-month clamping rule.

Result

Use the sample state or enter your values, then run the tool.

What this tool does

It performs pure calendar arithmetic without time zones or hidden business-day rules.

How to use it

Select the date, whole-number amount, unit and add/subtract direction. The tool shows the exact convention used.

Worked example

31 January 2024 plus one calendar month becomes 29 February 2024 because the target month has no day 31 and the tool clamps to month end.

Supported formats

Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD plus whole days, weeks or months.

Assumptions

A week is exactly seven calendar days. Month operations preserve day-of-month when possible and otherwise clamp.

Limitations

This is civil date arithmetic. It does not skip holidays/weekends and does not model elapsed hours across DST changes.

FAQ

Does normal use transmit my data?

No. The production intent is browser-side processing with no account, worker call or third-party transmission.

How are ambiguous dates or times handled?

The tool exposes the relevant convention, calendar variant, time zone or clamping rule and returns an explicit warning or error when a silent guess would be misleading.
