Excel Serial Number to Date Converter

Convert Excel 1900- or 1904-system serial values, preserve fractional-day time, and explicitly surface Excel’s compatibility-only serial 60 phantom date.

Result

Use the sample state or enter your values, then run the tool.

What this tool does

It reproduces Excel serial-date conventions instead of applying a generic day offset that breaks around serial 60 or the 1904 system.

How to use it

Enter the numeric serial and choose the workbook’s date system. Fractional values are treated as fractions of a 24-hour day.

Worked example

In the 1900 system, serial 59 maps to 1900-02-28, serial 60 is Excel’s non-Gregorian compatibility phantom 1900-02-29, and serial 61 maps to 1900-03-01.

Supported formats

Positive Excel serial numbers with optional decimal fraction; 1900 and 1904 date systems.

Assumptions

Serial 1 is 1900-01-01 in the 1900 system and 1904-01-02 in the 1904 system, consistent with Microsoft’s documented 1,462-day offset.

Limitations

Excel’s serial 60 cannot be represented as a real Gregorian JavaScript Date. The tool preserves it as an explicit labeled compatibility value.

FAQ

Does normal use transmit my data?

No. The production intent is browser-side processing with no account, worker call or third-party transmission.

How are ambiguous dates or times handled?

The tool exposes the relevant convention, calendar variant, time zone or clamping rule and returns an explicit warning or error when a silent guess would be misleading.
