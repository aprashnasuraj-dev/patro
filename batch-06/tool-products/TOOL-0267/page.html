What this tool does

Look up bounded joist and dimension-lumber beam spans from AWC DCA 6 assumptions, including the actual-span L/4 overhang limit and explicit out-of-scope warnings.

How to use it

Enter measured project values, review any product or code assumptions, then use the primary calculation action. Inputs are processed in the browser and bounded before calculation.

Worked example

For Southern Pine 2×8 joists at 16 in o.c. with a 10 ft actual span, the tool reports the DCA 6 joist limit, L/4 overhang cap and the selected beam span for the 10-ft joist category.

Supported formats and units

Inputs are explicitly labeled. Unit conversions use exact SI/customary conversion factors where applicable; product coverage values remain user-configurable.

Assumptions

Values are a direct bounded lookup of AWC DCA 6 Table 2 and Table 3A under their stated assumptions. Actual joist overhang is also capped at one quarter of entered main span.

Limitations

This is not structural engineering, permit approval or a general span calculator. DCA 6 is limited to its stated deck geometry, loads, materials and conditions; the authority having jurisdiction controls.

FAQ

Does this send project data to a server?

No during normal use. The production intent is browser-side execution with no account or upload.

Can this replace a code-compliant design or product takeoff?

No. Where structure, electrical safety, HVAC sizing, jurisdiction rules or manufacturer coverage matter, the tool exposes assumptions and warnings rather than claiming approval.
