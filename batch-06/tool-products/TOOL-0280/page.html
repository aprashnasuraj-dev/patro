What this tool does

Convert Nepal hill and Terai land units alongside square feet, square metres, acres and hectares, and decompose totals into Ropani–Aana–Paisa–Daam and Bigha–Katha–Dhur.

How to use it

Enter measured project values, review any product or code assumptions, then use the primary calculation action. Inputs are processed in the browser and bounded before calculation.

Worked example

Entering 1 ropani returns 5,476 ft², 16 aana and its metric/acre equivalents; entering 1 bigha returns 72,900 ft² and 20 katha.

Supported formats and units

Inputs are explicitly labeled. Unit conversions use exact SI/customary conversion factors where applicable; product coverage values remain user-configurable.

Assumptions

The Nepal conversions use published government relationships: 1 ropani=5476 ft²=16 aana=64 paisa=256 daam; 1 bigha=72900 ft²=20 katha=400 dhur. Metric values derive from the international foot.

Limitations

Legal parcel records, cadastral survey values and document-specific rounding control real transactions. Do not replace a licensed survey or land-record measurement with this converter.

FAQ

Does this send project data to a server?

No during normal use. The production intent is browser-side execution with no account or upload.

Can this replace a code-compliant design or product takeoff?

No. Where structure, electrical safety, HVAC sizing, jurisdiction rules or manufacturer coverage matter, the tool exposes assumptions and warnings rather than claiming approval.
